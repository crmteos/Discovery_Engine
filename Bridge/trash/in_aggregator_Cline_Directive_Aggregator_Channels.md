## 🎯 Executive Objective

Establish strict architectural isolation for communication channels between the **Knowledge Aggregator** (`05_Knowledge_Aggregator`) and the Execution Engine (**Cline**). This prevents context pollution between core mathematical directives (`01_Formal_Core`–`04_Discovery_Engine`) and higher-level analytical directives generated by the Knowledge Aggregator's Architect and OSINT Orchestrator.

---

## 📐 Channel Architecture

Refactor the `Bridge/` folder and `05_Knowledge_Aggregator` workspace into dedicated sub-channels:

```
Bridge/
├── in/
│   ├── core/         <-- Core Engine Directives (Modules 01–04)
│   └── aggregator/   <-- Knowledge Aggregator Directives (Module 05)
└── out/
    ├── execution_reports/   <-- Core Engine Reports & Benchmarks
    └── aggregator_reports/  <-- Aggregator Architect & Orchestrator Reports
```
